# The Deep Ones 0.24 — Living Environment

Public playtest for Windows and Linux (64-bit).

- Twenty-two edited recordings bring distinct water, oars, wind, rain, Norwegian woodland birds, a distant Finnish loon recording, coastal birds and harbour foley.
- Small firefly fields appear beside suitable freshwater banks on calm nights. Daylight, rain and strong wind suppress them. These are an intentional storybook accent.
- Sedge responds to wind; lamp moths, one chimney, wet-eave drips and moving fenders add restrained shore activity.
- Water gains local rain rings, occasional twilight rises, rock-contact spray, wet-line drops and faint saltwater oar/wake glow on suitable nights.
- Existing horror events can briefly disturb ordinary nature, while gameplay rules and permanent Deep One progression remain intact.
- Environment volume is independent of fishing effects. Nature and music yield to important fishing cues. Credits name the recordists, licenses and edits.

Download the platform archive, extract everything, and start `TheDeepOnes.exe` or `Start-Linux.sh`. No account is required. GitHub's automatic source archives contain only the public download page.

Existing 0.23 saves load with the new environment preference enabled. Chosen mute settings are preserved. Earlier saves retain the existing forward migration. The field book, protected finds, campaign and supplied music are retained.

Scoped Linux area/effect, settings, sound output/mute and fishing/book/Continue checks passed.
